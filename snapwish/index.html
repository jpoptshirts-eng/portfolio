Snapwish

Snapwish is a wishlist and group gifting app designed to make gift planning easier. It helps people discover products, save items, vote with friends and buy gifts from external retailers.

Main features

  • Snap a photo of an item to find similar products
  • Search products from supported retailers
  • Create personal wishlists
  • Create group gift lists
  • Vote on gift options
  • Let contributors claim or mark items as purchased

Purchases and retailers

Users can take a photo of a product they like, search for similar items, save products to a wishlist, invite friends, vote on gift options and coordinate who is buying what.

Snapwish does not process payments in the MVP. When users choose to buy an item, they are taken to the relevant retailer website, such as Amazon, to complete the purchase securely.

How Amazon links are used

Snapwish may show Amazon product links when users search for items or save gift ideas, so they can view details and complete purchases on Amazon when they choose to.

Snapwish may display affiliate links to products from Amazon and other retailers.

As an Amazon Associate, Snapwish earns from qualifying purchases.

Product categories

Users may browse or save ideas across many kinds of gifts and products, including home gifts, tech, headphones, bottles, trainers, beauty, fashion, toys, birthday gifts, leaving gifts and Christmas gifts. Actual availability depends on supported retailers and search results.

App status

The app is currently in prototype and MVP development, with dedicated iOS and Android releases planned.
